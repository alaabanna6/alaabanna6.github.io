About Me

Based in Toronto, Canada

Every design decision has a reason.

I'm a product designer, and I believe good design starts with understanding people. My process combines UX research, information architecture, and high fidelity prototyping. Everything is based on real user needs, and I use AI automation throughout.

AI automation is part of how I work every day. I use it to come up with ideas faster, build better workflows, and ship more on my own than a designer usually could. I follow new AI tools closely because what they can do changes every week.

I have a Master of Digital Media from Toronto Metropolitan University, where my research focused on product design, AI, and human experience.
