About Me

Based in Toronto, Canada

I'm a product designer, and I believe good design starts with understanding people. I take a product from first research to a tested prototype. I run usability tests and measure the results, build design systems that keep a product consistent as it grows, and prototype full flows. Accessibility is part of the work from the start.

AI automation is part of how I work every day. I use it to come up with ideas faster, build my own agents and workflows, and ship more on my own than a designer usually could. I stay current with new AI releases and put the useful ones to work quickly. I also believe in keeping a human in the loop, so the judgment and the final decision are always mine.

I have a Master of Digital Media from Toronto Metropolitan University, where my research focused on product design, AI, and human experience.
